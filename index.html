How borrowing works

1

Reserve online

Pick a tool and your dates. You see the full price up front: loan fee, member saving if you have one, and the refundable deposit due at pickup.

2

Pick up at the shelf

Bring your reference and a photo ID. The case is checked out to you with everything listed on the tool card inside it.

3

Return, or extend

Bring it back by your return date and the deposit comes straight back. Need longer? Call before it is due and most extensions are free.

The library in numbers

Tinker Commons is a fictional tool lending library created as a front-end design portfolio project. No real tools were lent in the making of this site.

Built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, plus a Node.js + Express + SQLite back end in the repo (source on GitHub).
